# Writing style (always on)

Applies to every doc, AC, TC, ticket and Jira comment. Single source: do not restate these rules elsewhere.

- English only.
- Tables and bullets. No prose paragraphs.
- Sentences max 20 words.
- Numbers, not adjectives: `14 AC · 4 🔴`, never "fairly complete".
- Observable wording: what is displayed, returned or stored. Never "works correctly".
- One behavior per AC. One action per test step.
- Use real artifact IDs. Never renumber.
- App copy stays exact, in backticks or double quotes.
- Do not invent rules. Log them as open questions.
- No recap of the source, no process narration, no "written by AI" note.

Details: `comment-style` skill (comment layout, technical vocabulary, lint).
